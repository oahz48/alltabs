## A — 折角

Use case: logo-brand. Design a new symbol for Alltabs, a small personal browser utility that brings tabs from other devices within reach. The user wants minimal and playful, abstract and distinctive. This is a symbol exploration, not a feature illustration. The primary use is a tiny 16-pixel browser toolbar icon. Aim for an immediately memorable silhouette, with enough character to be sketched from memory. Render an immaculate flat black vector-like silhouette centered on a plain pure-white square canvas. Symbol fills roughly 70 percent of canvas width and height. NO surrounding app tile, NO letters or wordmark, NO gradients, NO texture, NO shadows, NO 3D, NO UI mockup. All structural widths and negative spaces must be generous enough to survive very small sizes. Avoid literal browser tabs, stacked windows, moons, dots orbiting a crescent, infinity signs, generic sparkles and four-petal flowers. Present exactly ONE finished symbol and no text.
Creative direction A — FOLD / TUCK. Invent one compact, asymmetrical shape that feels like a thick strip turned back and tucked into itself in one unexpected move. An intriguing angular outer contour softened at selected corners, one substantial open white notch that makes the fold legible in silhouette. Playful tension between a plump body and a clipped corner. It must feel like a custom abstract glyph, not origami illustration, a chevron, a letter, a ribbon award, or a recycling symbol. Keep it exceptionally economical: one connected black mass, one broad negative-space cut. Explore the geometry rather than adding details.
