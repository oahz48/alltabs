What you can do with Alltabs

Browse your other devices

Find the tabs you left on your laptop, desktop, or phone, grouped by device in one side panel.

Find a page fast

Search page titles and addresses across visible devices. Jump straight to the tab you have in mind.

Open one tab or a whole batch

Open a single page, select a few, or open all tabs from a device on the computer you’re using now.

Copy the links you need

Select tabs and copy their links, one per line, ready to paste into notes, a message, or a project document.

Choose which devices you see

Hide devices you don’t need and restore them in settings. Keep your panel focused on what matters.

Keep your place as you browse

The side panel stays open as you open tabs, preserving your search and position. Device expansion choices are remembered.

Local processing. No upload to Alltabs.

Alltabs processes tab titles, URLs, searches, and selections locally on your device. It does not upload this data to the developer, the Alltabs website, or any developer-operated server. There is no Alltabs account, analytics, tracking, advertising, or remote code.

Tabs from other devices are supplied by Chrome's own sync service. Alltabs reads the snapshots Chrome makes available; it does not operate a separate sync service. Website icons use Chrome's built-in favicon service, and opening a link connects your browser to that website. These Chrome services and destination websites have their own privacy behavior.

Read the privacy policy →

System requirements

Desktop Chrome 141 or newer on Windows or macOS. Sign into the same Chrome profile on your devices with history and open-tab sync enabled. Phones supply tabs through Chrome sync and do not need Alltabs installed.

Tab availability and freshness depend on Chrome sync. Alltabs cannot force another device to sync or close remote tabs.
