Your tabs, within reach

Click a tab to open it here. Your list stays in place. Use Ctrl/Cmd-click or middle-click to open it in the background.

Select tabs to open several at once or copy their links. Device menus let you open all tabs or hide a device.

Missing a device?

Use the same Chrome profile and enable history and open-tab sync on each device. Open Chrome on that device and give it time to sync, then refresh here.

Alltabs reads Chrome’s latest snapshot every 30 seconds while visible. Refresh cannot force another device to sync. Chrome shares up to 25 windows per device.

Using OneTab

Open selected tabs here, then use OneTab’s own menu to collect them. Alltabs cannot close remote tabs or send tabs directly into OneTab.
